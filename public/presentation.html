Key insight
0px

The size grid never moves.

The photo, font and price can load late. If they have no space saved, they push the sizes down as you tap. So I saved their space ahead of time.

What I learned

Four things this build taught me.

01

Build for slow internet.

On a slow phone, a jumpy page leads to wrong-size orders. Each one means a return and a second delivery.

02

Measure speed with Lighthouse.

It turns "fast" into numbers I can check: the score, the layout shift and the button size.

03

Make targets big enough for a thumb.

Buttons are 48 px tall with 16 px between rows, so a tap never lands on two sizes.

04

Design for every state.

In stock, low stock, sold out and not launched yet each need their own look. Sold out stays in place, crossed out.

Four things I learned. Build for slow internet. Measure speed with Lighthouse. Make targets big enough for a thumb. And design for every state, not just in stock.
